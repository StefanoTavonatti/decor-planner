## [0.1.0] 2026-10-16

### Added

- Installable, offline-capable PWA with an update prompt, responsive and touch-friendly on phones and desktop.
- Wall setup from a photo with four-corner perspective correction and real dimensions, or as a blank wall; obstacles on the wall.
- Piece library shared across projects: outer frame dimensions, optional image, placeholders and hanging configuration.
- Wall composer with to-scale rendering, zoom and pan, snapping, gap readouts, eye-level guide, multi-selection, layering, locking, warnings and undo/redo.
- Mounting plan export as a to-scale PDF with nail coordinates, plus a PNG preview.
- Metric (mm, cm, m) and imperial (in, ft-in with fractions to 1/16") units for display and input.
- Local autosave of projects, backup reminder, and `.decorplan` export and import.
- Drag and drop of wall photos and piece images.
- Automatic deployment to GitHub Pages when a `vX.Y.Z` release tag is pushed.
- This changelog.
